# AP Calculus

**Hook:** Every May, roughly 450,000 US high schoolers sit a College Board exam that can turn a year of calculus into college credit. Two exams, one shared syllabus, and a score sheet going back to 2002.

## Two exams, one course family

- **AP Calculus AB** covers roughly a first semester of college calculus: limits, derivatives, integrals, and the [[Fundamental Theorem of Calculus]].
- **AP Calculus BC** covers AB plus more: series, parametric and polar curves, and more integration. BC takers also get an AB subscore.
- **Format (2027 exam):** a hybrid digital exam. Multiple choice is taken in College Board's Bluebook app; free-response answers are still **handwritten** on paper. Each half counts for 50%. Some parts ban calculators; others require a graphing calculator.

## What changes for 2026-27

- **Content: none.** College Board says the course "has not changed." Its Fall 2026 clarifications rewrite two lines, including the wording of the **[[Glossary#Extreme value theorem|Extreme Value Theorem]]**.
- **The multiple-choice section gets shorter, starting May 2027:** Part A (no calculator) goes from 30 questions in 60 minutes to **29 in 62**. Part B (calculator) goes from 15 questions in 45 minutes to **13 in 38**.

## The score record

Share of exams scoring **3 or higher** (often the cutoff for college credit), from College Board's published distributions:

| Year | AB | BC | AB takers | BC takers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2002 | 67.3% | 81.2% | 157,524 | 41,785 |
| 2019 | 58.4% | 81.0% | 300,659 | 139,195 |
| 2021 | 51.0% | 75.2% | 251,639 | 124,599 |
| 2023 | 58.0% | 78.5% | 273,987 | 135,458 |
| 2024 | 64.4% | 80.9% | 278,657 | 148,191 |
| 2025 | 64.2% | 78.6% | 285,891 | 160,436 |
| 2026 | 65% | 82% | not yet listed | not yet listed |

- **BC takers nearly quadrupled** between 2002 and 2025, while AB less than doubled.
- **The 2024 jump in AB.** In 2024 College Board recalibrated scores in several subjects using a method it calls "evidence-based standard setting." Its own brief lists AB and BC among the exams that did *not* get a score change. The AB 3-or-higher rate still rose about 6 points that year, mostly in 4s. College Board's materials checked here do not explain that jump.
- **Credit policy varies.** Each college decides what score earns credit, if any.

## The new on-ramp: AP Precalculus

Launched in **2023-24**, AP Precalculus is College Board's most popular course launch, by its own count. More than **184,000** students took the first exam, and **76%** scored 3 or higher. College Board data reported by Education Week say it reached more rural, first-generation, Black, and Hispanic students than AP Calculus AB or AP Statistics. Of students who took it before senior year, **60%** took AP Calculus the next year. These figures come from College Board, which also runs the exam.
