# Widening Access to Calculus

**Hook:** Calculus is a gateway course, and getting to it depends a lot on where you go to school. A cluster of 2026 projects aims at that gap, with free courseware, volunteer tutors, and AI.

## Who is doing what (2026)

| Project | What it is | Status, as reported |
|---|---|---|
| **Learnvia** | Nonprofit collaborative launched by Carnegie Mellon University. Courseware for college gateway math: lessons, homework, quizzes, forums, and a built-in **AI tutor**. | Calculus I described as **free to students**, announced Jan 29, 2026. Calculus II listed for **Fall 2026**, Calculus III "coming soon." No outcome data published on its site. |
| **MIT4America Calculus Project** | MIT undergraduates and alumni give weekly **remote, human** tutoring to students in under-resourced high schools. Began with a summer 2025 camp; supported by the Siegel Family Endowment. | 14 districts and about 37 tutors as of March 2026, with a stated goal of about 20 districts. Evidence so far is teacher testimony, not measured results. |
| **Khan Academy** | Free online practice and video. | A **revised AP Calculus AB** course with new videos, exercises, and free-response practice, announced Aug 2026. |

## What is not known yet

- **Whether these work at scale.** None of the three has published independent evaluations of learning outcomes.
- **The AI-tutor question.** How well an AI tutor handles calculus specifically is still lightly studied; see [[Calculus and AI#Where chatbots stumble]].
