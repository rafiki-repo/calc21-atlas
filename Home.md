## Calculus in one paragraph

Calculus is **two ideas and one surprise**. The first idea: you can measure *how fast something is changing right now* (the **derivative**). The second: you can add up *infinitely many infinitely small pieces* to get a total (the **integral**). The surprise: these two are secretly **inverse operations**, which is the [[Fundamental Theorem of Calculus]]. Everything else, from physics to machine learning, grows out of that.
