# Free Calculus Textbooks

**Hook:** A full calculus sequence, from limits to vector calculus, can be read for free, legally, and some of it can be edited and remixed.

## The two most common picks

| | **OpenStax Calculus** (Vols 1–3) | **Active Calculus** |
|---|---|---|
| **Who** | Senior contributors Gilbert Strang (MIT) and Edwin "Jed" Herman (UW–Stevens Point) | Matt Boelkins and collaborators (Grand Valley State University) |
| **Style** | Conventional textbook: explanation, worked examples, exercises | Built for **active learning**: short previews and activities that students work *before* the explanation |
| **Coverage** | Vol 1: limits to applications of integration. Vol 2: integration techniques, intro differential equations, series. Vol 3: vectors, multivariable, vector calculus | *Active Prelude to Calculus*, *Active Calculus* (single variable, 2nd ed.), *Active Calculus Multivariable* (2nd ed.) |
| **Extras** | Low-cost print copies | Free interactive **WeBWorK** exercises in every section; Runestone and Doenet versions; instructor groups |
| **License** | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (no commercial reuse) | CC BY-SA (source on GitHub; can be forked) |

## Recent changes

- **Active Calculus**, 2026: a print edition of the single-variable 2nd edition (January), print workbooks and a version with revised previews and exercises (August), and an HTML and Runestone release of the multivariable 2nd edition (August), according to the project's news posts.
- **OpenStax**: no new edition found as of this check.

## Good for / not good for

- **Good for:** self-learners, courses that avoid textbook costs, and instructors who want to adapt material (Active Calculus' license is the more permissive of the two).
- **Not good for:** anyone wanting a single integrated video course. For that, see [[MIT OCW Single Variable Calculus]].

## Access

- [OpenStax Calculus Volume 1](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/preface): web and PDF, free, no login
- [activecalculus.org](https://activecalculus.org/): HTML free, links to all versions
