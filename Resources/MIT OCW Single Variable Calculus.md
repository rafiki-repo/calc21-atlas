# MIT OCW Single Variable Calculus

**Hook:** MIT's first-semester calculus, packaged for people who are not at MIT.

## What you get

- **18.01SC Single Variable Calculus** (Fall 2010), taught by **David Jerison**, rebuilt as a "Scholar" course **designed for independent study**.
- Lecture videos with notes, recitation videos, worked examples with solutions, problem sets and exams **with solutions**.
- Interactive "Mathlets" demonstrations.

## Good for / not good for

- **Good for:** self-learners who want structure (lecture, then practice, then check), and as a companion to a free text.
- **Not good for:** anyone wanting graded feedback or a certificate. It is a course archive, not a live course. Some interactive pieces were built with older technology.
