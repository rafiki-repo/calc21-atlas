# Lean Companion to Analysis I

**Hook:** An undergraduate real analysis course, from building the real numbers to the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, with every definition and theorem written so a computer can check it.

## What you get

- A Lean version of [[Terence Tao]]'s textbook *Analysis I*, intended as a **faithful paraphrase** that accompanies the book rather than replacing it.
- Coverage includes the construction of the reals, limits, series, continuity, **derivatives** (including L'Hôpital's rule), **Riemann integration**, and the [[Fundamental Theorem of Calculus]].
- Exercises are left as blanks (`sorry`) for readers to fill in. The author does not plan to post solutions.

## Good for / not good for

- **Good for:** readers who already know some calculus and want to see exactly what a rigorous proof requires, and anyone curious what "formalized math" looks like.
- **Not good for:** a first pass at calculus. It also requires some Lean setup and gets more out of you if you have the book.

## Access

[teorth.github.io/analysis](https://teorth.github.io/analysis): free, open source on GitHub, no login. Lean can be tried in the browser via the [Lean community site](https://leanprover-community.github.io/).
