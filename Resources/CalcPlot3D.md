# CalcPlot3D

**Hook:** Multivariable calculus is hard to picture on a flat page. This browser app lets you grab a surface and spin it.

## What you get

- A free JavaScript app by **Paul Seeburger** (Monroe Community College), developed with NSF support (grant DUE-IUSE 1524968). Older versions were a Java applet, which is why some old pages mention plug-ins.
- Plots surfaces, space curves, **vector fields**, parametric and implicit surfaces, and regions, all rotatable in 3D.
- Built-in tools for topics in Calc III: partial derivatives, gradients, Lagrange multipliers, double and triple integrals, and line integrals. Mostly checkboxes and sliders, with no coding needed.
- A direction-field explorer for differential equations, and **STL export for 3D printing** surfaces.

## Good for / not good for

- **Good for:** Calc III students and instructors, and anyone who wants to see a [[Glossary#Gradient|gradient]] or a saddle point rather than imagine one.
- **Not good for:** symbolic algebra or step-by-step solutions. It shows; it does not solve.
