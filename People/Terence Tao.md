# Terence Tao

**Hook:** A Fields Medal-winning analyst at UCLA who has become one of the most closely followed voices on how AI and proof assistants fit into real mathematics.

## Known for

- **Analysis research** across harmonic analysis, PDEs, and number theory. He received the Fields Medal in 2006.
- ***Analysis I* and *II***, widely used undergraduate textbooks that build calculus rigorously from the ground up.
- **Math and AI in practice:** he released a Lean companion to *Analysis I* (2025), co-authored the AlphaEvolve exploration paper (2025), and co-set the Strong Prime Number Theorem formalization challenge with Alex Kontorovich.
- **New in Nov 2026:** a popular-math book, *Six Math Essentials* (published Nov 10, 2026), and **Lean Grimoire**, a browser logic game built with a coding agent that can show the Lean proof behind each move. He is currently seeking playtesters.
- **Measured public commentary** on AI-generated math. Recent coverage quotes him warning that people prompting AI may not understand its proofs well enough to defend them.
