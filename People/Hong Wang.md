# Hong Wang

**Hook:** The analyst who, with Joshua Zahl, settled a century-old question about turning a needle around in 3D space, and a 2026 Fields Medalist.

## Known for

- **2026 Fields Medal**, announced July 23, 2026 at the International Congress of Mathematicians in Philadelphia. The citation covers **harmonic analysis** and **geometric measure theory**: Fourier restriction, local smoothing for the wave equation, Falconer distance sets, Furstenberg sets in the plane, and the Kakeya problem in three dimensions.
- **The 3D [[Kakeya Conjecture]]**, proved with **Joshua Zahl** (UBC) in a 127-page arXiv preprint posted February 2025. The medal went to Wang alone; the proof is joint work.
- Affiliations: **NYU Courant Institute** and **IHES** (France). PhD advisor: Larry Guth.

> "I got interested in the Kakeya problem from Fourier analysis." (interview with *Plus*, July 2026)
