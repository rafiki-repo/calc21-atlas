# Levent Alpöge

**Hook:** His July 2026 post on X began "hello there the jacobian conjecture is false thanx". Then came a polynomial map that broke an 87-year-old conjecture. In November he claimed the Riemann hypothesis.

## Known for

- **The Jacobian counterexample (Jul 20, 2026):** an explicit degree-7 map in three dimensions that disproves the [[Jacobian Conjecture]] in every dimension from 3 up. He credited Anthropic's Claude model with work that led to it, and Akhil Mathew with suggesting the problem. The map is short enough that other mathematicians checked it quickly.
- **Forced Euler blow-up (Sep 2026):** with **[[Tristan Buckmaster]]**, an AI-assisted, Lean-checked claim that the forced Euler fluid equations can blow up. The result is **claimed, not yet peer reviewed**. It built on the strategy of [[Cordoba and Martinez-Zoroa|Córdoba and Martínez-Zoroa]]. See [[Navier-Stokes Smoothness Problem]].
- **The Riemann hypothesis (Nov 20, 2026):** a four-page paper and about 17 million lines of Lean, "working with Claude," claiming the [[Riemann Hypothesis]] and its generalization to Dirichlet L-functions. The Lean FRO and Mathlib Initiative certified the Lean proof of the classical hypothesis on Nov 21. The argument is **not yet understood or peer reviewed** by humans. Earlier, in Aug 2026, he and Ralph Furman checked a Claude result on the share of zeta zeros on the critical line.
- **Before AI:** a number theorist. He wrote papers on counting points on curves and on which integers are sums of two cubes, several of them with Manjul Bhargava and Ari Shnidman.

## Career in brief

- **Harvard:** AB in mathematics. Later a Junior Fellow of the Harvard Society of Fellows (2021-2025).
- **Princeton:** PhD 2020, thesis *Points on Curves*, advised by Manjul Bhargava.
- **Honors listed on his CV:** the AMS-MAA-SIAM Morgan Prize for undergraduate research, and an asteroid named after him (25898 Alpöge).
- **Now:** described as a mathematician at Anthropic (as of 2026 press coverage).
