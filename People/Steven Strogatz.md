# Steven Strogatz

**Hook:** A Cornell mathematician who wrote the chaos textbook many students meet first, then spent the next decades telling the rest of us why calculus is worth caring about.

## Known for

- ***Infinite Powers*** (2019): the history of calculus told as a story for general readers. A *New York Times* bestseller.
- ***Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos*** (1994): a widely used textbook on differential equations and chaos.
- **Other books:** *Sync* (2003), *The Calculus of Friendship* (2009), *The Joy of x* (2012), and *Big Math* (2026, with Alex Townsend).
- ***The Joy of Why***: a *Quanta Magazine* podcast he has hosted since 2022.
- **Role:** Susan and Barton Winokur Distinguished Professor for the Public Understanding of Science and Mathematics at Cornell (chair created in 2023).
- **Recognition:** elected to the National Academy of Sciences (2024), SIAM's George Pólya Prize for Mathematical Exposition (2019), and the National Academies' Schmidt Award for science communication (2023), among others listed on his site.
