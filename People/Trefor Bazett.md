# Trefor Bazett

**Hook:** A teaching professor who turned his calculus classes into free "video books," then put the same lessons on YouTube for everyone else.

## Known for

- **The "Dr. Trefor" YouTube channel:** the public home for most of his video-book lessons, plus topics beyond calculus. A 2024 news post on his prize put the channel at over 408,000 subscribers.
- **Video books:** open online course books (no login) built mostly from his videos, with learning objectives, short summaries, and exercises before and after each video. Two cover calculus:
  - *Calculus 1: Video Modules*: limits through integration techniques, written for UVic's Math 100 as a companion to the APEX Calculus textbook. The page says the versions linked here are not monetized.
  - *Introduction to Vector Calculus*: from an asynchronous online course in Spring 2021, covering roughly the first third of UVic's Math 204.
- **Watch first, then class:** the modules are meant to be watched *before* each lecture, with the instruction to pause and answer the questions.
- **Role:** Associate Teaching Professor of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Victoria (since 2019, per his site). PhD from the University of Toronto.
- **Recognition:** 2024 **PIMS Education Prize** from the Pacific Institute for the Mathematical Sciences, for contributions to mathematics education. His site lists nine teaching awards in total, through a 2025 UVic Faculty of Science award.
