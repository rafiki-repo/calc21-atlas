# Grant Sanderson

**Hook:** The creator of 3Blue1Brown, whose animated videos have become a common first stop for people who want to *see* why calculus works.

## Known for

- **3Blue1Brown**, a YouTube channel (with written and interactive versions on its site) that explains math visually. Its stated goal is to "make more people love math." It does not run sponsorships in its videos and is supported through Patreon.
- ***Essence of Calculus*** (2017), a video series that starts from the area of a circle and aims to make viewers feel they "could have invented calculus."
- **Neural-network lessons**, including a chain-rule walkthrough of backpropagation. See [[Backpropagation and the Chain Rule]].
- **Manim**, the open-source Python animation library he wrote for the channel. A separate, community-maintained edition is the version recommended for newcomers.
- **Summer of Math Exposition** (since 2021), an event that encourages others to make math explainers.
- **Background:** studied math and computer science at Stanford, then made multivariable-calculus content for Khan Academy before focusing on 3Blue1Brown from late 2016. He has also lectured for a semester at MIT.
