# Yu Deng

**Hook:** A PDE specialist who works on a question David Hilbert posed in 1900: can the equations of fluids be *derived*, step by step, from billiard-ball particles obeying Newton's laws? A 2026 Fields Medalist.

## Known for

- **2026 Fields Medal** (announced July 23, 2026), for work in **partial differential equations**. Based at the **University of Chicago**; a researcher in the Simons Collaboration on Wave Turbulence.
- **Particles to Boltzmann:** with Zaher Hani and Xiao Ma, a rigorous derivation of the **Boltzmann equation** from hard-sphere dynamics over long times, extending Lanford's 1975 short-time result (arXiv 2408.07818).
- **Boltzmann to fluids:** a March 2025 preprint by the same trio claims to complete the chain to the Euler and Navier-Stokes-Fourier equations, addressing part of [[Hilbert Sixth Problem|Hilbert's sixth problem]]. See that note for its status.
- **Wave turbulence:** derivations of *wave kinetic equations* from nonlinear dispersive equations, and probabilistic methods for the nonlinear Schrödinger equation.
