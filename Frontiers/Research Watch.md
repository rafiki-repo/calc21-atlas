## Prizes to watch

- **Fields Medal** (every 4 years, at the ICM). **2026, Philadelphia:** [[Hong Wang]] (harmonic analysis) and [[Yu Deng]] (PDEs), plus John Pardon (symplectic geometry) and Jacob Tsimerman (number theory and algebraic geometry). The same ceremony gave the **Leelavati Prize** for outreach to Hannah Fry.
- **Salem Prize** (yearly, IAS): for young researchers in analysis. The 2026 cycle took nominations until September 1, 2026; winners not yet seen as of last check.
- **Abel Prize** (yearly): 2026 went to Gerd Faltings for arithmetic geometry, outside calculus' orbit.

## Where to watch

- [arXiv math.CA](https://arxiv.org/list/math.CA/recent) (classical analysis and ODEs) and [math.AP](https://arxiv.org/list/math.AP/recent) (analysis of PDEs): where new results appear first, unrefereed
- [*Plus* magazine](https://plus.maths.org/), for readable interviews around major prizes
