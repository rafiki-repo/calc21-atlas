## Prizes to watch

- **Fields Medal** (every 4 years, at the ICM). **2026, Philadelphia:** [[Hong Wang]] (harmonic analysis) and [[Yu Deng]] (PDEs), plus John Pardon (symplectic geometry) and Jacob Tsimerman (number theory and algebraic geometry). The same ceremony gave the **Leelavati Prize** for outreach to Hannah Fry.
- **Clay Research Awards** (announced Apr 14, 2026): Tuomas Orponen, Pablo Shmerkin, [[Hong Wang]], and Joshua Zahl, for harmonic-analysis work that proved the Furstenberg set conjecture in the plane and the Kakeya conjecture in 3D; and [[Yu Deng]] and Zaher Hani, for deriving the Boltzmann equation from hard spheres. (A third award, in topology, went to Burklund, Hahn, Levy, and Schlank.) Clay's **Mirzakhani New Frontiers Prize** went to Anna Skorobogatova for geometric measure theory.
- **King Faisal Prize in Science, Mathematics** (Jan 2026): [[Carlos Kenig]], "for his contributions to the theory of nonlinear partial differential equations."
- **Salem Prize** (yearly, IAS): for young researchers in analysis. The 2026 cycle took nominations until September 1, 2026; winners not yet seen as of 2026-11-26.
- **Abel Prize** (yearly): 2026 went to Gerd Faltings for arithmetic geometry, outside calculus' orbit.
