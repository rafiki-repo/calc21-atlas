# Stories

The history, feuds, and turning points behind calculus. Back to [[Home]].

## Before Newton
- [[Archimedes Palimpsest]] — Archimedes' slicing method, which survived under a prayer book; a lost page found in 2026
- [[Ibn al-Haytham]] — the volume of a sphere from stacked cylinders and a sum of squares, around 1000
- [[Kerala School]] — Madhava's series for π, sine, and cosine around 1400, and the long fight to get them noticed

## The founders and after
- [[Newton vs Leibniz]] — the priority dispute, and the committee report Newton wrote himself
- [[Nonstandard Analysis]] — infinitesimals: used by the founders, mocked by Berkeley in 1734, made rigorous by Robinson in the 1960s
- [[Weather Forecasting]] — includes Richardson's 1922 "forecast factory" of 64,000 human computers
