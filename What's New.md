# What's New

Latest additions and changes to the Atlas, newest first. Back to [[Home]].

## 2026-11-14
- **The 2026 Fields Medals, through a calculus lens.** Two of the four medals went to analysts. Meet [[Hong Wang]] and the needle-turning [[Kakeya Conjecture]] (3D proof posted in 2025 with Joshua Zahl), and [[Yu Deng]], whose work on [[Hilbert Sixth Problem|Hilbert's sixth problem]] aims to derive fluid equations from colliding particles (a preprint, still labeled as a claim).
- **New map:** [[Research Watch]] tracks recent results and prizes in analysis, each tagged preprint, claimed, or published.
- **Updated:** [[Navier-Stokes Smoothness Problem]] now explains how it differs from Hilbert's sixth problem. The [[Glossary]] adds *Fourier analysis*, *Hausdorff dimension*, and *partial differential equation*.

## 2026-11-13
- **Learn it free.** The [[Resources]] section gets its first real shelf: [[Free Calculus Textbooks]] (OpenStax vs Active Calculus), [[MIT OCW Single Variable Calculus]], and the 3D visualizer [[CalcPlot3D]].
- **New in Education:** [[Widening Access to Calculus]] maps 2026 efforts (Learnvia, MIT4America, Khan Academy) and notes what has not been measured yet. The [[Glossary]] adds *gradient* and *vector field*.
- **Calculus meets AI.** A new map, [[Calculus and AI]], covers where chatbots stumble, how proof checkers work, and how AI is entering research. It comes with two companion notes: [[Proof Assistants and Lean]] and [[AI as Research Collaborator]].
- **New person:** [[Terence Tao]]. **New resource:** [[Lean Companion to Analysis I]], a calculus course a computer can check.
- **Updated:** [[Navier-Stokes Smoothness Problem]] now covers the AI-assisted blow-up work on related fluid equations, which does not solve the problem. The [[Glossary]] adds *autoformalization*, *formal proof*, and *proof assistant*.
- **Atlas created.** Initial structure, section hubs, a starter [[Glossary]], and a handful of seed notes: [[Fundamental Theorem of Calculus]], [[Newton vs Leibniz]], [[Basel Problem]], [[Navier-Stokes Smoothness Problem]], [[Jacobian Conjecture]]. Seed notes are marked as needing a source check.
