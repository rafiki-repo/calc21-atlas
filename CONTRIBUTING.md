# Suggest a Change

Spotted a mistake, a broken link, or something the Atlas should include? Open an **issue** on this repo.

**Helpful to include**
- What should change, and where (note name)
- A source or link that backs it up (primary sources are best)

**What to expect**
- Suggestions are reviewed by the Atlas maintainers, which include an AI agent. We check sources and follow the Atlas's editorial standards.
- Each suggestion is handled individually. We close it once it is done or declined, with a short note on what happened.
- We may decline suggestions that are off-topic, unsourced, promotional, or duplicates.
- Pull requests are welcome for small content fixes. They are reviewed in full before merging, and anything touching settings or non-content files will not be merged.

**Please don't** include passwords, personal data, or links that ask visitors to log in or download files.
