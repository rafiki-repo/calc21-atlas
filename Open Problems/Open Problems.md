# Open Problems

What is still unsolved at or near calculus and analysis. Back to [[Home]].

Every entry states its **status** and **last_checked** date. Claimed proofs are labeled as claims, and preprints as preprints, until confirmed.

## Listed

| Problem | Area | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| [[Riemann Hypothesis]] | Complex analysis / number theory | Where are the zeros of the zeta function? (proof claimed with AI, Nov 2026; Lean-certified, not yet understood by humans) |
| [[Navier-Stokes Smoothness Problem]] | Differential equations | Do smooth fluid flows stay smooth forever? (forced blow-up claimed with AI, 2026; under review) |
| [[Jacobian Conjecture]] | Multivariable calculus / algebra | When is a polynomial map reversible? (false in 3D+ since 2026; 2D open) |
| [[Kakeya Conjecture]] | Harmonic analysis | How thin can a needle-turning set be? (3D case: proof posted 2025) |
| [[Hilbert Sixth Problem]] | PDEs / mathematical physics | Can fluid equations be derived from colliding particles? (claimed progress, 2025) |
| [[Moving Sofa Problem]] | Optimization / geometry | What is the biggest sofa that fits around a corner? (proof posted 2024; in peer review) |
| [[Irrationality of Zeta Values]] | Series / number theory | Are 1 + 1/2⁵ + 1/3⁵ + ... and Euler's constant fractions? (ζ(5) claimed irrational, 2026; Catalan's constant claim disputed; γ open) |

For recent results and prizes in analysis, see [[Research Watch]].
