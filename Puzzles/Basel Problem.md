# Basel Problem

**Hook:** Add up 1 + 1/4 + 1/9 + 1/16 + ... forever. The total is finite, and it is exactly π²/6, a number that comes from circles.

## The problem

What is the sum of the reciprocals of the perfect squares, 1/n² for n = 1, 2, 3, ...? It was posed in the 17th century. Leonhard Euler solved it in the 1730s, finding the sum equals π²/6 (about 1.645).

## Why people love it

- Nothing about the sum mentions circles, yet π appears.
- Euler's original approach was bold and not fully rigorous by modern standards; later proofs made it airtight, and many different proofs exist.
