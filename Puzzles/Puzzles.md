# Puzzles

Famous integrals, series, and problems to play with, plus the competitions that turn them into a sport. Back to [[Home]].

## Classic puzzles

- [[Basel Problem]]: an infinite sum with a surprising answer, π²/6
- [[Borwein Integrals]]: seven integrals equal π/2, the eighth misses by 10⁻¹¹
- [[Sophomores Dream|Sophomore's Dream]]: ∫ x⁻ˣ from 0 to 1 equals 1 + 1/2² + 1/3³ + ..., and it's true
- [[Gaussian Integral]]: no antiderivative, yet the area under the bell curve is exactly √π

## Tricks of the trade

- [[Feynman Integration Trick]]: differentiate under the integral sign, a tool Feynman popularized and Leibniz invented

## Competitions

- [[MIT Integration Bee]]: speed integration, live on stage, every January, plus bees from Florida to Lisbon
- [[Putnam Competition]]: six hours, twelve proofs, an AI entrant in 2025, and four shorter sessions from Dec 2026 (plus a 2025 problem to try)
